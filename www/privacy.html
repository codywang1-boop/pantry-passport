Pantry Passport – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Pantry Passport helps you find dishes from the ingredients you have. We keep data use to the minimum the app needs.

What stays on your phone

Your ingredients, saved dishes, your own recipes and shopping lists are stored only on your device. We have no account system and do not collect your name, email, contacts or location.

AI features (Premium)

When you use AI dish ideas, More dishes, or photo scanning, the ingredient list or the photos you choose are sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes them to Anthropic's Claude AI to produce the answer. Photos and text are used only to answer that request. We do not keep them or use them for advertising. Our server keeps a short-lived count of requests per network address to prevent abuse.

Camera and photos

The app uses the camera or photo picker only when you tap a photo button. Photos are not uploaded unless you ask the app to read them.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. You can cancel anytime in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/codywang1-boop/pantry-passport or contact the developer through the Google Play listing.
